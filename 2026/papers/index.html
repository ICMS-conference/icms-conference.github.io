Proceedings Papers

This volume contains the proceedings of the International Congress on Mathematical Software (ICMS) 2026, held in Waterloo, Ontario, Canada, from July 20 to 23, 2026, hosted jointly by Wilfrid Laurier University and the University of Waterloo. ICMS is a biennial meeting devoted to all aspects of mathematical software, bringing together developers and users of computer algebra systems, numerical software, and formal proof tools. The 2026 edition featured sessions on automating mathematical reasoning through interactions between proof assistants, computer algebra systems, and generative AI; algebraic solvers for polynomial systems; computational category theory; parallel methods, AI, and applications of computer algebra in physics; mathematics in Lean; high-performance computer algebra; computing methods in (dual) quaternion matrix/tensor computation; software for enumerative and analytic combinatorics; biological applications of computer algebra; design and implementation of computer algebra systems and software; mathematical knowledge management; computational methods in combinatorial algebra and geometry; symbolic-numeric computation; and a general session.
